# Notices

## obra/superpowers

feature-coordinator follows the packaging conventions of
[obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers): harness-agnostic
skills, a bootstrap skill injected at session start, per-harness tool
mappings, the polyglot `run-hook.cmd` dispatcher pattern and the
per-ecosystem manifests. `hooks/run-hook.cmd`, the output-shape logic in
`hooks/session-start` and `scripts/bump-version.sh` are adapted from that
project.
